Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours. We don't collect, store, or sell your personal information. Ever.

Our Privacy Promise

SoloThinking is built on a simple principle: your data belongs to you. We don't collect it. We don't store it. We don't sell it. Unlike most websites that track your every move, we've designed SoloThinking to work completely without any personal data collection.

When you use our Life Expectancy Calculator or any other tool, nothing leaves your browser. Your age, gender, lifestyle choices — all of it stays on your device. We never see it. We never store it. We have no servers logging your inputs.

What Data Do We Collect?

Almost nothing. Here's the complete list:

  • Anonymous usage statistics — We use privacy-friendly analytics (no cookies, no IP tracking) to count how many people use our tools. We don't know who you are, where you're from, or what answers you gave.
  • Essential technical data — Your browser sends standard information (like page load time) to help us fix bugs and improve performance. This data is anonymized and never linked to you.

What we DO NOT collect: Your name, email address, IP address, location data, answers from our calculators, browsing history, or any personally identifiable information.

Cookies & Tracking

SoloThinking uses no tracking cookies. None. Zero. We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other tracking scripts. The only cookies that may be stored are strictly necessary for the website to function (like remembering your preferences). We don't use these for advertising or analytics.

You can use every tool on SoloThinking completely anonymously — no cookie banners, no "accept tracking" popups, no hidden trackers.

Third-Party Services

We use a few third-party services that may process anonymous data:

  • Cloudinary — For image optimization and delivery. They may collect anonymous usage data.
  • Cloudflare — For security and performance. They may see anonymous request data.
  • Font Awesome & Google Fonts — For icons and typography. These services may collect standard web request data.

None of these services receive any personal information from you. They only see that someone visited solothinking.com — not who you are or what you did on the site.

Contact & Email

If you email us at xabdullahsaleem3@gmail.com, we will receive your email address and message. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry. We never add your email to any marketing lists or share it with third parties.

We retain email correspondence for as long as necessary to address your question, and then delete it. We do not build email databases or send unsolicited emails.

Children's Privacy

SoloThinking does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Our tools are educational in nature and do not require any personal data to function. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

Your Rights

Since we collect virtually no data about you, there's not much to request access to. However, if you have any concerns or questions about your privacy on SoloThinking, you have the right to:

  • Ask what data we have about you (answer: almost none)
  • Request deletion of any data we may hold
  • Opt out of any data collection (there's nothing to opt out of)
  • File a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, simply contact us.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date below. Any changes will be minor and will never introduce data collection without your knowledge. We believe in radical transparency — if something changes, you'll know about it.
