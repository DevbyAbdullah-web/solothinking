Frequently Asked Questions

What is a life expectancy calculator?+
A life expectancy calculator estimates how long you will live based on your age, biological sex, and lifestyle factors such as smoking, exercise, sleep, and stress levels. SoloThinking free life expectancy calculator uses WHO and CDC actuarial data combined with 7 science backed health factors to give you a personalized lifespan estimate in under 2 minutes with no signup required.
How long will I live?+
The global average life expectancy is 73 years according to WHO 2024. 71 years for men and 76 years for women. However, your personal lifespan depends heavily on lifestyle choices. Non smokers who exercise regularly, sleep 7 to 9 hours, and maintain strong social connections can live 10 to 15 years longer than average. Use our free life expectancy calculator above to get a personalized estimate based on your specific health profile.
What is the most accurate life expectancy calculator?+
The most accurate free life expectancy calculators combine WHO and CDC actuarial tables with personal lifestyle factors including smoking status, exercise frequency, sleep duration, stress levels, and social connections. SoloThinking calculator incorporates 7 evidence based variables validated by research in The Lancet and JAMA making it one of the most comprehensive and accurate free longevity tools available online.
What is a longevity quiz and how does it work?+
A longevity quiz also called a life expectancy quiz or lifespan test asks you a series of questions about your health and daily habits then calculates your estimated lifespan based on scientific research. SoloThinking free longevity quiz takes under 2 minutes, requires no account or signup, and gives you a personalized result with actionable tips to add years to your life. It is based on WHO and CDC data.
Is there a life expectancy calculator based on medical history?+
Yes. A life expectancy calculator based on medical history factors in your health conditions, lifestyle habits, and biological data to estimate your lifespan. Our calculator uses 7 validated health variables like smoking, exercise, sleep, stress, social connections, biological sex, and current age. All sourced from WHO and CDC research. This approach gives a science backed personalized estimate without requiring private medical records or clinical data.
How do I take a how long will I live quiz?+
To take a free how long will I live quiz, scroll up and click our Life Expectancy Calculator. You will answer 7 questions about your age, biological sex, smoking status, exercise habits, sleep, stress, and social connections. The quiz takes under 2 minutes and gives you your estimated lifespan in years, your days remaining, your probability of reaching age 90 or 100, and a personalized action plan with tips to add years to your life.
What is a longevity test?+
A longevity test is an online assessment that estimates how long you are likely to live based on your health, lifestyle, and behavioral habits. Unlike a clinical test, a longevity test requires no blood work or medical records. It uses research validated questions about factors like exercise, sleep, smoking, and stress. SoloThinking free longevity test is based on WHO and CDC data and takes under 2 minutes to complete.
